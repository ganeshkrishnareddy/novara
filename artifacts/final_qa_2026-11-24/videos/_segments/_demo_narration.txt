NOVARA product demo one point zero. This is a local product walkthrough using synthetic QA data. No real customer record, message, campaign or external integration is involved. The demo opens on the command center, then shows one shared customer identity in contacts. Leads move through qualification and conversion without any outbound communication. Campaign Studio demonstrates draft and scheduling controls only; no send is performed. The customer inbox, projects and work item planning show the shared audit spine and workspace isolation. Advanced experimentation runs local statistical analysis, and the agent center shows a simulated run held at a human approval gate. The accompanying QA report records limitations openly: local SQLite rather than a production Postgres deployment, no mobile installers, no hosted external providers, and screen capture at nineteen twenty by one thousand eighty.
