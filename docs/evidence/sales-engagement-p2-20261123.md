## Delivery boundary (important)

Sellbird is AGPL v0.4.0+ / MIT ≤0.3.x; nothing was copied. The signal
vocabulary is a clean-room governed catalog of 29 signals (26 free, 3 paid)
defined locally. There is no broker, no outbound email, no real delivery and
no external AI provider: drafts and offers come from deterministic local
templates (`generated_by: local-template`, `external_model_calls: false`),
and the durable outbox's only terminal send state is an explicitly labelled
`simulated` dry run (`external_delivery: false`). Approval is the default for
every message; autopilot requires a confirmed opt-in.
