# NOVARA brand assets

This directory holds the launch brand kit for NOVARA, one self-hostable business operating system: CRM, sales, marketing, support, feedback, projects, documents, automation, and governed AI.

All artwork here is **original and locally generated** for this launch. It contains no third-party artwork, no emoji, no external fonts, no external images, and no network-fetched assets. The SVGs use vector primitives only (no raster embeds). The PNGs were rendered locally with Python Pillow and system-installed Segoe UI font files.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Swatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest green (primary) | `#176B55` | primary field, mark, favicon ground |
| Gold (accent) | `#C4A66A` | node joins, rules, secondary text |
| Dark text | `#192D27` | wordmark on pale and white surfaces |
| Pale background | `#F7F8F7` | light surfaces and light-variant text/mark |
| White surfaces | `#FFFFFF` | cards and panels |

Use only these colors in the logo marks. The social cards and poster may use translucent overlays derived from the dark text and forest green values for depth.

## Type

- Stack: `"Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` — system fonts only, no downloads.
- Wordmark: all caps, bold, tight letter-spacing (`-1.6` at 42 SVG units).
- Tagline: **"Everything Connected. Everything Under Control."**
- Supporting line: **"Open-source business operating system · Local-first"**

## Mark

The mark is an original geometric monogram: four connected nodes forming an **N**, where the four nodes stand for separate capabilities operating as one connected system. The two diagonal joins carry a subtle gold accent. The geometry is defined in a 48-unit grid with 5-unit strokes and 5-unit-radius nodes so it stays crisp at 16px favicon size and at 1200px display size.

## Usage rules

- Give the logo clear space of at least one mark width on all sides. Do not crop, rotate, stretch, recolor, or add effects to the mark.
- Use `novara-logo.svg` on pale/white surfaces and `novara-logo-light.svg` on dark forest-green surfaces. Never place the dark variant on a dark field, or the light variant on a white field.
- Do not place the horizontal logo below 120px rendered width; use the favicon mark alone for small applications.
- Keep the gold accent as a join and rule detail only; it is not a primary text color on pale backgrounds.
- Do not redraw the mark, substitute a different font, or replace the node network with generic AI/technology imagery.
- The poster caption identifies the source honestly as a local QA capture with synthetic data; do not present it as customer footage or live production data.
